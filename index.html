About

A background
in systems.
A focus on
people.

I'm a dual MDes/MBA candidate at IIT with a background in architecture. After four years studying buildings, I spent two more actually building them — working on federal projects for the U.S. Army and Air Force, where I became less interested in the drawings and more interested in the systems behind them: how decisions get made, how workflows break down, how people experience the spaces we were designing.

That curiosity pulled me toward design research and strategy. Now I'm bridging architecture's spatial intuition with business thinking to understand how people experience the world — and where those experiences could be better.
